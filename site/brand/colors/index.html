A continuous flow

Deep ink, a clean blue action color, and a blue-to-teal gradient along the drawing stroke.

Recommended starting point

03 · Ocean Ink

Deep ink provides the structure. Blue gives actions a consistent, visible role. The blue-to-teal stroke adds movement to the chosen mark without filling the interface with decoration.

The cost: the gradient needs an SVG export and a solid fallback. Keep buttons, permission labels, and state indicators solid. Choose Architect Blue if restraint matters more than a distinctive color signature.

What each color does

A palette with clear jobs.

Canvas & surfaces

Quiet backgrounds and raised panels. They support the drawing, its labels, and the people using it.

Ink & hierarchy

Headings, body text, and secondary labels. Confidence starts with information that is easy to read.

Actions & selection

Buttons, links, focus, and selected items. The accent should identify an action consistently.

Brand & states

A gradient can belong to the logo. Success, warning, errors, and permissions keep their own labels and semantic treatment.
