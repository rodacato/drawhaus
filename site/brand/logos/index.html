Direction to refine

G · Freehand dh

F's initials become a softer, freer drawing gesture. G keeps the d and h readable while adding the bowed stem and relaxed curves suggested by the reference.

The deciding test: recognize the initials at favicon size without relying on color. Choose H for a more expressive signature, or I for the simplest single-letter mark. Use the Drawhaus wordmark at a first encounter.

This is a recommendation, not an adopted identity. “Give ideas a home” and the lowercase wordmark are proposed treatments. The product name remains Drawhaus.

What each asset is for

One mark, different jobs.

01

Symbol

Favicons, app icons, small navigation spaces, and avatars. Use the mark alone where the name would be too small.

02

Lockup

App header, landing page, and README. Pair the symbol with the Drawhaus name for a first encounter. The lockups above are previews.

03

Share image

Link previews for the product site. Combine the final mark, a clear product description, and a real screenshot. Keep shared diagram previews separate.

Downloads in this round are symbols only. Final wordmarks, favicon files, and social images follow the logo choice.
